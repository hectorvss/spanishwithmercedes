DESCRIPTION (EN)

You land at a hotel in Spain and real Spanish doesn't sound like the textbook. In this video I get you ready to survive (and enjoy) any hotel situation in Spanish: how to book a room, exactly what to say at check-in and check-out, and how to ask for what you need without sounding rude — using the conditional tense to make polite requests, the way a native speaker actually does.
